Parallel Waters — Routing Claude Code's Parallel Work

2026 | Teaching Site

A teaching site for Claude Code's agents and parallel-work features, written for the people who have to explain them—sales engineers and technical AEs. Instead of a feature-by-feature tour, it's built as a spine of routing questions ("would this side work drown my conversation?", "do the workers need to talk to each other?") drawn as a branching river map, with each feature as the answer to a question. Every technical claim traces back to captured documentation, and where the docs are silent, the site says so.

On Record — Chicago '27 Mayoral Housing Tracker

2026 | AI Pipeline with Human Oversight

An autonomous daily pipeline that tracks what Chicago's 2027 mayoral candidates say about housing: discovery → Whisper transcription → LLM extraction. Built around oversight—a second model from a different family verifies every quote and attribution, a human approves every publish, and every claim links to its source. A working study in AI reliability: cross-model verification, human-in-the-loop review, and auditable provenance.

Youth to Senior: Do Youth World Cups Feed the Senior Team?

2026 | Data Investigation

An investigation into whether youth World Cups actually develop senior internationals, measuring the share of each senior World Cup squad that came through a nation's U-17 or U-20 teams. Covers 17,481 player squad entries across 184 senior squads over six tournaments (men's 2010–2022, women's 2019–2023), with player identities matched through Wikipedia squad lists and Wikidata. Key finding: how often a federation reaches youth tournaments matters far more than how well it converts the players it sends.
